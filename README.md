# Of Me — guildrysantana

This is my **Of Me**: a living, portable record of who I am — my people, places, and
what matters to me — so any AI I use can understand me instead of starting from zero.
It is **mine** — it lives in my own GitHub, and I can take it anywhere.

Hydration happens by talking with a connected, write-capable AI (ChatGPT or Claude),
ideally by voice — it reads this world and helps me fill it in over time.

## The map

Four sections, and they are the same four I see at
[ofme.ai/house](https://ofme.ai/house) — because it is the same house, read two ways.
[`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) is the exhaustive version: every file by path.

**From the desk** — written *for* me, by my guide.

- [Afterwords](afterwords/README.md) — what it writes back after reading something I said.
- [Letters](letters/README.md) — written for one person, never sent without me.
- [Playlists](music/README.md) — made from what I told it, with a reason for every track.

**Rooms** — my life itself. One folder per subject, and most of them don't ship: they
appear as I fill them.

- [About me](about-me/about.md) — interests and what's on my mind — and
  [my guide](about-me/guide.md), who I chose and the person behind it.
- [Stories](stories/README.md), [people](people/README.md), [lists](lists/README.md)
  and [music](music/README.md) — the four that come with a contract already in them.
- [Domains](domains/README.md) — a subject I've started talking about that
  hasn't earned a room of its own yet. New subjects land here first.

**The entryway** — the informal half of the house. Nothing here is an index,
nothing here was written for me, and none of it is filed.

- [My shelf](stories/shelf.md) — anecdotes I've parked to tell another day.
- [My drawer](drawer/drawer.md) — passing fragments, one line each; nothing is sorted at intake.
- [My lists](lists/README.md) — what I mean to get to.
- [Poolside](poolside/README.md) — scenes to step into, when a question is the wrong shape.

**The foundation** — what the rest stands on: when things happened, and the raw voice
and text everything else was made from.

- `timeline/` — dated events, in order, each one materialized once and referenced after.
- [My sources](sources/README.md) — every recording, import and contribution, with what
  each one can be trusted to carry. **Every claim in this house traces back to something
  in there.**

And the parts that are machinery rather than rooms:

- [The deck](about-me/deck.md) — eight piles of questions. I say "show me the deck", pick
  the piles I'm in the mood for, and get asked one at a time.
- [Agent instructions](AGENTS.md) — how a connected AI should read, converse, and update this world.
- [Sensitivity preferences](sensitivity-preferences.md) — how I want sensitive things handled.
- [Follow-ups](about-me/follow-ups.json) — threads my AI noted to pick back up later.

## Fixing what's wrong in here

Everything above is readable at [ofme.ai/house](https://ofme.ai/house), and it is
**editable right where I read it** — the title, the details at the top of a record, and
every paragraph. I click the words and type; it saves to this repository by itself,
one commit per change, no branch and nothing to merge. Most of what ends up in here was
written *about* me by an AI, so being able to correct a sentence without opening GitHub
is the difference between a record I trust and one I stop reading.

The parts of a file that Of Me writes and keeps current for me — the section between the
`OF-ME:BEGIN`/`OF-ME:END` markers in this file and a few others — are marked read-only
there, because they get regenerated whenever the tuning improves. If I'd rather own one
of those files outright, I can click it and say so; that file then stops receiving
updates, and a line at the top of it says so and how to undo it.

## When a subject earns its own room

The folders above ship with every Of Me. **The interesting ones don't.** A subject that
keeps coming up in my life eventually outgrows being a file inside another folder, and
at that point it should become a folder of its own — a room — named after the subject
and nothing else.

**A new subject starts in [my domains](domains/README.md), not at the top of the
house.** Something that has come up once or twice gets a folder in there —
`domains/film/`, `domains/cooking/` — and lives in it until there is enough to justify
a front door. That folder is the waiting area, and the contract inside it carries the
full rule.

**A room is earned, and the test is that the records already exist.** Not that the
subject seems important, and not that a room would be tidy: a room is earned when there
are several records about it that are awkward where they currently sit, and it is
created by MOVING those records into it — everything in its `domains/` folder included,
so the subject ends up in one place rather than two. A folder made in advance of its
contents is a filing cabinet somebody else labelled — it invites an AI to manufacture
records to fill the drawers, which is exactly backwards. **Nothing here should exist
because a structure expected it to.**

**Whatever a record says it is, it is.** `record_type` in the front matter decides how
a record is treated, never the folder it sits in. That is what lets a room hold an
ordinary note and a machine-read record side by side without either being mistaken for
the other.

## The shelf

When something comes up that I don't have time to get into, I say **"shelf that"**
and it lands on [my shelf](stories/shelf.md). When I say **"let's see the shelf,"**
Utah pulls a few and I tell one — and it gets written down in `stories/`,
in my words, for good.

Shelve it now, tell it later, keep it forever.
