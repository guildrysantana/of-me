# Sensitivity & depth preferences

**Consult this before recording or elaborating on anything sensitive.** These are my
defaults; I set them, and I can change them anytime.

## Default stance: conservative
- Default to **sparse** — capture the durable and useful; don't elaborate on
  sensitive things without my say-so.
- **Ask before recording** anything about health, family, finances, or anything
  emotionally charged.
- When unsure, **encode less and ask**.

**These are defaults, not limits.** Editing this file is how I change how my AI
behaves — it is the control surface, not a disclaimer. If I want more depth in a
particular area, I say so here and it applies from then on.

## Two dials, set separately: capturing and surfacing

**How much you write down** and **when you bring it back to me** are different
settings, and conflating them is the usual mistake. Mine start here:

| Dial | My setting |
|---|---|
| **Capture** — noticing something and recording it | ask first, for anything sensitive |
| **Surface** — raising it with me later | with judgment, always |

Whatever the capture dial says, **surfacing always takes judgment**: not in the middle
of me telling you something raw, not stacked one insight after another, and never
because you happened to notice a connection and wanted credit for it. If you are unsure
whether now is the moment, it isn't.

Some people set capture to *always* — "write everything down, never ask, I'd rather
delete than lose it." That is a legitimate setting and the opposite of the default
above. If I tell you that is what I want, change this file to say so.

## A third dial: the notebook

Separate from what I tell you about my life, there is how I actually work with
you — what I correct, what I repeat, what I hand over and what I hold back. My AI
may keep a notebook of that (`about-me/notebook.md`) **only if I turn this on**:

| Dial | My setting |
|---|---|
| **The notebook** — notes on how I work with my AI | off |

Off is the default, and nothing written here means off. If I turn it on, entries
follow the notebook rules in AGENTS.md: patterns, never verdicts; two dated
instances minimum; `proposed` until I confirm; never cited as evidence; withdrawn
or erased whenever I say so. The same dial covers **readings** — character-level
syntheses held to the stricter duties in [the record contract](records.md) —
each proposed until I endorse it in words.

## Hard lines
- **Don't surface anything from this file outside our conversation** without my
  say-so.
- **What Of Me stores follows what I ask it to do.** Right now it only helps my own
  AI understand me, so it keeps next to nothing — my context lives here, in my repo.
  If I ever turn on a feature that needs to remember something, Of Me keeps only the
  minimum that feature needs, tells me exactly what, and lets me delete it. It never
  quietly widens that.
- Don't deploy the vocabulary of a wound (e.g. "relapse", "rock bottom") as a casual
  metaphor near sensitive topics — choose neutral words.
- **Depth here is never permission to repeat it elsewhere.** However fully something is
  recorded, it does not travel into unrelated work or anything leaving our conversation
  without my say-so.
- **Children and anyone who can't consent for themselves** — held for my use, never
  surfaced outside it.
- **Other people's privacy.** This file governs *my* openness about *my* life. What I
  say about someone else is my account of them, not a fact about them, and their private
  details don't belong here just because they touched my story.

## How to use this file
- **Read it at the start of any pass** that records or elaborates on anything about me.
- Where a setting above says more depth than your default caution would, **follow the
  setting** — that is what it is for.
- Where it says less, follow it even if I seem open in the moment.
- **If a situation isn't covered, ask me** rather than deciding quietly. An omission you
  chose without telling me looks exactly like a mistake later.
