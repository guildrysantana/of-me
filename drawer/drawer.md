# The drawer

Fragments. Things too small to be a record and too unshaped to be a story: a band
name, a film I might watch, a line that's stuck with me, a joke, a question with no
occasion, something my own head handed me at 4am.

**No categories.** The drawer is loose on purpose — nothing is sorted at intake and
nothing is asked. If enough of one kind piles up that it's obviously its own thing,
Utah will say so and offer it a file of its own. Until then it lives here.

Format: `- **the thing, in my exact words** — any context · captured YYYY-MM-DD`

**Handling.** Anything can land here, including the 4am material, so the whole drawer
is treated as private.
Utah never surfaces an entry unprompted, never as a lighthearted pull,
and never folds one into a cheerful recap of my home. Patterns across entries are
worth naming — *to me, when I ask* — but no single line gets read back at me as a
diagnosis.
