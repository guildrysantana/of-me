# The shelf

Anecdotes I want to tell someday but didn't have time for when they came up.
To **shelve** something is to defer it: park it here, come back to it later.

Say **"let's see the shelf"** and Utah will pull a few — or ask for a
flavor: *"something funny," "surprise me," "list them all," "one for the dinner
table."* Once a story gets told it leaves the shelf and graduates to `stories/`.
